# The Switchyard: optional first-play sheet

Status: **Not measured.** No human playtest, enjoyment score, human completion time, microphone test, or real Voice test is claimed. The 8-12 minute first-play target is a design intention. Automated run durations do not measure human puzzle solving.

Invite three to five first-time players only with their agreement. Let each use the local Practice mission without a developer command script. Do not record, recruit externally, upload, or add analytics without separate consent. Let players stop whenever they wish.

Before playing, ask: "What do you think your job is, and what can Pip do?" Avoid explaining the solution. Keep the human manual and reported local evidence available.

After playing, ask:

1. What made you choose the lift or the maintenance bypass?
2. Which report from Pip changed what you did with the panel?
3. Did you change plans? What made that possible or confusing?
4. When was the difference between draft and applied routing clear or unclear?
5. Where did confirmations help, and where did they feel repetitive?
6. What was the most frustrating moment? Could you recover without help?
7. Would you try the other approach, and why?

If a player agrees to timing, distinguish reading/discussion, manipulating the panel, travelling/confirming, and recovery. Count repeated requests, unwanted proposals, missed confirmations, and facilitator interventions only when directly observed. Do not infer feelings from speed or automate an enjoyment score.
