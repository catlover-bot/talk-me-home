# Optional first-play check: The Switchyard polish

Status: **not measured**. No participants have been recruited or recorded. Optional human availability does not block the implemented delivery.

Invite three to five consenting first-time players to try local Practice, with normal controls and no developer solution. Let them choose an approach and stop whenever they wish. Do not record or upload their session without consent.

Ask after play:

1. Before Apply, what did you expect the draft to change? What did the applied panel actually confirm?
2. Which information from Pip did you use with the manual? Was any report hard to find or easy to mistake for a current reading?
3. Why did you choose your approach? Did anything make you change your plan?
4. Where did you repeat an action, lose your place, or need help? Could you find the next exact confirmation and Pause?
5. Did the departure reflect the approach you completed? Would you want to try the other approach, and why?

Record participant answers verbatim or clearly mark facilitator summaries. Separately note task comprehension, comparison steps, recoveries and voluntary route changes. Fewer clicks or shorter automated runs are not evidence of enjoyment. The 8-12 minute design intention remains unmeasured until an actual human session supports a duration report.
