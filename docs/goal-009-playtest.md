# Optional first Switchyard play

Status: not measured. No participants, quotations, scores, retention figures or enjoyment results have been collected. The roughly three-minute first useful decision is a design target, not a timer or measured play duration.

With a consenting first-time player, open local Practice and let them choose The Switchyard. Let them use or skip the opening and optional help. Do not provide a developer solution, require recording, or change the public service.

Ask after they play or choose to stop:

1. What was unclear about your role, Pip's role, or the next interaction?
2. Which actual observation changed your plan or a routing decision?
3. Where did you feel you were thinking, and where were you merely repeating instructions?
4. Could you distinguish a draft, acknowledged routing and a separately confirmed physical action?
5. Did help let you investigate, or did it tell you too much or too little?
6. Would you try the other approach? Why or why not?

Keep answers verbatim or clearly label facilitator summaries. Record optional observations separately from subjective answers. Do not interpret an automated completion, click count or solve time as enjoyment. Stop whenever the player wishes; no recruitment is required to deliver the implementation.
