# Optional human playtest — Talk Me Home Goal 006

This short future study tests design hypotheses. It is not a completed human playtest and is not required before local delivery. Record the actual build, mode, viewport and recorder selection. A Live study needs its own applicable authorization; otherwise use Practice and report that mode accurately. Do not infer enjoyment from clicks, session length, English fluency or synthetic self-play.

1. **After the opening, what do you think you control and what does Pip control?** Ask before explaining the rules. Note the first useful question/action and time to the first shared Cargo success; separate reading/thinking from technical waiting.
2. **Which reported fact changed your Gallery route, and where did you put that evidence?** Note whether the player distinguishes powered/open from clear/blocked/unchecked, and can cross out, undo or recover from a wrong direction without guidance.
3. **Did you choose to visit the archive, collect the recorder or leave it? Why?** Note what information supported the choice, whether this layout required an extra trip, and whether skipping felt like a valid rescue. Do not imply that collection is the correct choice.
4. **When you needed the map, latest report, proposal or Pause, could you reach it?** Record actual gestures, lost context, redundant permission questions, repeated inspections, and any conversation that stopped progressing. Separate purposeful planning from provider/server waiting.
5. **What did the ending acknowledge about your decisions, and would you choose anything differently next time?** Ask for an example rather than a rating. Note whether home was clearly confirmed and whether the visible recorder matched the committed pickup.

Keep short observations and exact voluntary quotes. Mark assistance, interruptions and technical faults explicitly; do not relabel faults as game difficulty or fabricate aggregate satisfaction scores.
